Why Florida Weather Rocks

  • Endless Summer — Beach season never really ends. Even "winter" days hover in the 70s.
  • Warm Gulf Stream Waters — The Atlantic and Gulf stay swimmable nearly all year.
  • Spectacular Sunsets — Humidity and ocean air create some of the best sunset shows in the country.
  • Afternoon Thunderstorms — Brief, dramatic summer storms clear out fast, leaving fresh air behind.
  • No Snow Shoveling — Ever.

Florida's Two Seasons

🌴 Dry Season (Nov–Apr)

Low humidity, mild temps in the 60s–70s, and clear blue skies — perfect for snowbirds escaping the cold.

🌊 Wet Season (May–Oct)

Warm, lush, and tropical with short afternoon showers that keep everything green and the beaches warm.
